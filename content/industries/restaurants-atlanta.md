## Your food is not the problem. The internet is.

Walk into a good Atlanta restaurant on a Friday and the room tells you everything: the light, the smell, the way the staff move. Now look that same place up on your phone. A menu trapped in a PDF, hours you are not sure are current, an order button that throws you to a third-party app that pockets a cut and keeps your customer. The gap between how a restaurant feels in person and how it reads online is where the money leaks out.

We are Range of View Studios, an Atlanta creative studio. We build brands, ordering-ready websites, menus, and content for restaurants and food businesses, and we build them to earn, not just to look good. We know it works because we did it for The Bando, and their online revenue doubled after the relaunch.

## The room already sells. The website should too.

Most restaurant sites are treated as a brochure: an address, a menu, a phone number, done. But your customer does not experience your restaurant as a brochure. They experience it as a feeling, and the feeling is your brand. When the website, the menu, the flyer, and the feed all carry that feeling, a first-time visitor trusts you before they have tasted anything. When they do not, you look like a template, and a template does not make anyone hungry.

So we start with the brand: the look and feel of the room, translated into a mark, a palette, and a type system that survives the trip from the front door to a phone screen. Then we build the site around one job, getting a hungry person from your profile to a placed order in as few taps as possible, with a menu that is always current and a path that keeps the customer yours.

## Stop renting your own customers

Here is the quiet tax most restaurants pay. Every order that runs through the big delivery apps is a customer you rent instead of one you keep, minus a fee off the top. You did the work to earn that loyalty. Someone else is banking the relationship. Over a year, that adds up to real money and a customer list you never get to build.

We design a direct ordering path on your own site, so the order, the margin, and the customer data stay with you. The apps can still exist as a supplement for discovery, but they stop being the only way in. Own the door, own the relationship.

## One brand, everywhere a guest finds you

A restaurant lives in more places than a website. There is the menu in the guest's hands, the flyer in the window, the post in the feed, the call that comes in mid-service. Each of those is a chance to feel like you, or a chance to feel generic. We build the whole set in one look: menus and print designed to photograph well and stay easy to update, illustration and brand art that carry the room's personality, content that makes the food look the way it tastes, and lead capture that turns a first visit into a standing habit.

## The calls you miss during service

Ask any operator what happens to the phone during a Friday rush. It rings, and nobody can get to it. Reservations, catering questions, someone checking the hours, all landing at the worst possible moment. Each one goes to voicemail, and a good share of those callers simply move on. That is bookings walking out the door while your kitchen is at full tilt.

This is where our creative-tech practice comes in. We can stand up an AI phone assistant that answers the calls you cannot, handles the routine questions, takes reservations and catering details, and passes the calls that need a human straight to you. It does not replace your hospitality. It stops your busiest hours from quietly costing you the next week's business.

## The Bando, in short

The Bando had the food and the following. What it lacked was an online presence that matched the kitchen. We rebuilt the brand and the site around how people actually order, tightened every step from profile to purchase, and let one identity carry across every surface. The result was a doubling of online revenue after the relaunch. The recipes did not change. The presence did.

## Built for Atlanta food

Atlanta's food scene moves fast and rewards personality. A brand that feels like a national template disappears here. We build for the specific city you operate in, the neighborhoods, the crowds, the way people find a new spot and decide in ten seconds whether to trust it. We would rather make something that could only be your restaurant, and let that be the reason the order goes to you.

If your kitchen is carrying a website that does not deserve it, let's fix that. Tell us where you think you are leaving money, and we will tell you what we would build.
