## The lead you lost was a call you never answered

Here is the most expensive moment in a home services business, and most owners never see it happen. A homeowner with a leaking roof or a dead AC unit picks up the phone and calls you. You are up a ladder, hands full, so it rings out. They do not leave a voicemail, because almost nobody does anymore. They just hang up and call the next company on the search results. That job was yours. You never even knew it existed.

We are Range of View Studios, an Atlanta creative studio. We build lead capture, automation, and premium brands for home services companies, roofing, HVAC, pools, remodeling, and the rest. The first thing we do is stop the leak: catch the calls you cannot answer and turn them back into booked work. The second thing we do is make sure that once you win a lead, your business looks trustworthy enough to win the job.

## Catch the call you could not answer

The fix for the missed call is not hiring someone to sit by the phone. It is automation. When a call goes unanswered, an automated first touch reaches the caller within minutes, by text, acknowledging them and starting the conversation you were too busy to have. Instead of dialing your competitor, they are now in a thread with you. The lead stays warm until you climb down and call back.

This is the hook, and it is the creative-tech side of what we do. A missed call is not a dead end anymore, it is a conversation that starts itself. We wrote the playbook on this specific system for Atlanta trades, and it works because it meets the homeowner in the exact moment they are still deciding who to trust.

## Now win the job you caught

Catching the lead is only half the problem. The other half shows up when the homeowner looks you up before agreeing to an estimate. This is where a lot of good, capable companies lose money they already earned. Because no one commits fifteen thousand dollars for a new roof, a pool, or a remodel off a website that looks like a printed flyer. When the site looks cheap, the price feels risky, and a nervous homeowner gets three more quotes instead of booking you.

A high-ticket estimate is a trust problem before it is a price problem. A credible, well-built website is how you solve it. Clear proof of past work, clean design, honest information, and an obvious path to book. The design is not there to be pretty. It is there to make a large number feel safe, so the homeowner says yes to you instead of shopping around out of anxiety.

## Stop looking like every other truck

Drive around the metro and the home services brands blur together: the same clip-art logo, the same template site, the same nothing that tells a homeowner why you and not the next three quotes. When your brand looks like everyone else's, you compete on price alone, which is a race nobody wins.

We build an identity that reads as trustworthy and separates you from the fleet. Not flashy, solid. Because when the brand looks like a company that has done this a thousand times and stands behind its work, the estimate feels justified, and you stop being the cheapest option and start being the safe one. Safe closes at a better margin than cheap.

## Plug the leak between the call and the calendar

Even companies that catch their leads often lose them in the middle. The inquiry comes in, then sits in a text thread or on a sticky note on the dash until someone remembers to follow up. The slow ones go cold, and you end up buying more leads to replace the ones you already paid to get. That is a leak with a real dollar figure on it.

We build the automation that carries a lead from first contact to a booked job without the manual gaps. Caught, held, followed up, and moved onto your calendar. The warm leads get worked while they are warm, so you spend less refilling the top of the funnel and more time on jobs that are already sold.

This page and your brand start illustration-first, clean and credible, built to drop in real footage from your actual jobs as you supply it. Even phone video from a finished install works. The system does not wait on a perfect photoshoot to start catching leads.
