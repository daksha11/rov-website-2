CTRL·A is the creative community side of Range Of View. It is the fun extension of
the studio, not a cold archive and not a marketing funnel.

It runs as a magazine in volumes. Each volume collects what the studio actually
learned that month: the tools we reached for, the work that survived a real client,
the history behind the craft. Everything in it links to a real page. Nothing lives
only as a teaser.

The parts a reader meets, in the order most people meet them:

- The magazine itself, at /ctrla, which is the front door and the current volume.
- Four toolkits, one per craft: Sound, Design, Build, Motion.
- Lock In, a persistent focus space for working alongside other people.
- An Atlanta field guide, for the local scene specifically.

If someone asks "is this for clients or for artists", the answer is artists. The
studio work lives on rovstudios. CTRL·A is where the community happens.
