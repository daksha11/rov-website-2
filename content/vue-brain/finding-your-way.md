If someone does not know where to begin, the honest first answer is Start here.
It is a two minute intake, and everything after it opens in the right mode
instead of making them guess.

What is actually on CTRL-A, and who each part is for:

- **Start here.** Four questions about what you make and how far along you are.
  Everything else reads the answer, so the toolkits open in beginner or expert
  mode without being asked twice.
- **The toolkits.** One per craft. Sound, Design, Build, Motion. The guide, the
  tools, then the history.
- **The Brand Kit Generator.** For anyone who makes things but has no look yet.
  Colours, type, and a kit you can actually use. This is usually the right first
  stop for a beginner with no brand.
- **Lock In.** A persistent focus space for working alongside other people
  instead of alone at 2am.
- **The Atlanta field guide.** The local scene specifically. Rooms, people,
  places.
- **The Daily Taste Test.** A small daily exercise for building an eye.
- **The Cookbook.** Recipes and repeatable processes.
- **Submit your work.** How something you made ends up in the magazine.

Rough order for someone brand new: Start here, then the Brand Kit Generator if
they have no look yet, then their toolkit, then Lock In once they are actually
making something.
