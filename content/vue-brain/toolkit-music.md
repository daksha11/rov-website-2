DAWs through distribution. What our engineers actually run, tested in real
sessions, in the order they reach for it. Nothing is on the list because
someone paid for it to be.

What a beginner needs to hear first: everyone starts by buying something, and
your room, your takes, and your ears will do more than the plugin list ever
does. Start there, then come back for the tools.

You will not hear the difference on the first pass. That is normal. Keep going.
