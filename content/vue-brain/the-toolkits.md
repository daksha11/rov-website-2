Four toolkits, one per craft. Each one runs the same three-part shape: the craft
guide, then the tool stations, then the history.

- **Music (Sound).** DAWs through distribution. What our engineers actually run,
  tested in real sessions.
- **Design.** Interface, brand, 3D, and the bits in between. Every pick has
  survived a real client.
- **Web Dev (Build).** Framework down to deploy, in the order you will meet them.
- **Video (Motion).** Bodies, glass, grip, then the room where it gets finished.

The rule behind every list: nothing is on it because someone paid for it to be.
These are the tools the studio uses, in the order it reaches for them.

Each toolkit has a beginner and an expert mode. Which one a reader gets by default
comes from the intake quiz at /ctrla/start, which records their craft and level.
