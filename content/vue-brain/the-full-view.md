The Full View is the name of what Range Of View sells. Five moments in a customer's
life with a business. Most shops sell one of them. We build all five so they feel
like one company.

| Moment | What breaks without us |
|---|---|
| Found | Invisible where customers actually search, including AI search |
| Captured | Traffic arrives, nothing happens, and no one knows why |
| Answered | The form sits in an inbox. Hours pass. The lead is gone |
| Nurtured | One email, then silence |
| Kept | The sale ends and the relationship ends with it |

The pitch in one breath: most people sell you one piece, a site or a funnel. We
build the full view, the site, the reply, and everything after.

The four practice areas (brand identity, web and product, video and motion,
creative tech and AI) are how we deliver it. They are capabilities, not the offer.
Lead with the Full View and let the practice areas explain the how.

The wedge is that the same studio does the brand work and the systems work, so
they arrive as one thing.
