Bodies, glass, grip, then the room where it gets finished. In that order, on
purpose.

What a beginner needs to hear first: the camera matters less than the light, and
the light matters less than knowing what you are pointing it at.

Cinematic is a craft, not a purchase. Shoot enough and you stop needing to be
told that.
