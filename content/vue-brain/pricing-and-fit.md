The floor is $2,500. We do not go below it, and there is no discount ladder. If
someone cannot get there, the honest answer is that we are not the right fit yet,
said warmly.

Shape of the work:

- Project builds start at $2,500 and run to about $4,500 for the entry tier, and
  $5,000 to $10,000 for the larger one, scaling with scope.
- Retainers run $1,000 to $3,000 a month, with no long lock-in.
- Media day rates are the one deliberate exception to the floor.

Projects are the entry, retainers are the goal. A build is how a client meets us.
The recurring work is the site staying current, the sequences being written and
tuned, and the automations maintained.

Who we serve: Atlanta and metro Atlanta businesses, owner-led or with a small
marketing team, where the owner is close enough to the money to feel a lead go
cold. Core industries are Beltline businesses, home services, real estate agents,
real estate developers, restaurants, and tech companies.

Who we are not for: businesses looking to buy traffic, anyone shopping purely on
price, and companies large enough to need an agency of record.
