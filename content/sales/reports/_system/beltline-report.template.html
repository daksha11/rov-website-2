No strings attached

Five fixes you can make this week, free

Whether or not we ever work together, do these. Each is backed by the data above and costs nothing but an hour.

  1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile, fully

    Every field: categories, hours, trail-side photos, attributes, description. Complete profiles get 2.7x the credibility and 50% more purchase consideration.8

  2. Answer every open question on your profile, then seed five of your own

    91% of owners never do.12 Parking, walk-ins, dogs, cards, reservations: ask and answer them yourself.

  3. Restart review velocity, and reply to every one

    A QR card at the register is enough. Recency now matters as much as rating, and AI reads the same reviews.3

  4. Add LocalBusiness schema and a plain-language About page

    State what you are, where you are (including "on the Beltline" and your segment), who you serve, in words a machine can quote.

  5. Get onto the lists AI actually reads

    Beltline.org's directory, Discover Atlanta, neighborhood guides, an active Yelp, plus Apple Maps and Bing. This footprint is what AI cites.6
