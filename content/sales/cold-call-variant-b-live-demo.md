# Cold Call · Variant B · The Live Demo

**Mechanism being tested:** demonstrate the problem instead of describing it. Open as the customer they just lost, then swing into proof, then book to show them the fix live.
**Caller:** hired SDR / rep.
**Goal:** book the 15-minute call live, to show the demo / teardown on screen.
**Destination offer:** free teardown or ready-made demo → 15-min call → The 14-Day Booking Site.

The 3 steps: **1) Hook (be the lost customer) → 2) Swing (testimonial OR demo line) → 3) Book to show them.**

---

## The one rule that makes this honest (and better)
Before you dial, **actually do the thing**. Really try to place an order on their site, book an appointment, or call the number after hours. Whatever wall you hit is your hook. Now you're not acting, you're reporting. When you reveal who you are, it lands as "I'm a real customer you just lost," not "gotcha." Reveal within about 15 seconds. Never drag the bit out.

If their site somehow works perfectly, don't force it. Skip to Variant A for that one, or find the next-weakest thing. The hook only works when it's true.

---

## Step 1 · The Hook (be the lost customer)

Pick the version that matches what actually broke when you tried:

**Hook 1a — couldn't order / book online:**
> Hi, yeah, I was just on your website trying to [place an order / book an appointment] and it wouldn't let me finish, kept [dead-ending / spinning / asking me to call]. Is there another way to do this?

**Hook 1b — called after hours, no way to self-serve:**
> Hey, I tried reaching you last night around 9 and couldn't get through, and I couldn't find any way to book on your site either. Do you take bookings online at all, or is it phone-only?

*(Let them respond once. They'll start to help you or explain. That reaction is the point, they're now feeling the exact friction their real customers feel.)*

**The reveal (within ~15 seconds, warm and honest):**
> Okay, I'll be straight with you [name], I'm actually not a customer, I'm [rep] from Range of View, we build websites for [industry] here in [city]. But I want you to notice what just happened: I genuinely tried to [order / book] on your site 20 minutes ago and I couldn't. I'm probably not the first person this week that happened to. That's real money walking out the door. Can I take 40 seconds to show you what I mean?

*(The reveal has to feel like a favor, not a trick. Tone: "I'm on your side and I just found a leak." If they're annoyed: "Fair, and I get it, but that little jolt of annoyance is exactly what your customer feels right before they book somewhere else. That's the whole problem I called about.")*

---

## Step 2 · The Swing (interchangeable proof)

Now pivot to credibility. Use **whichever you actually have** for this prospect. They're interchangeable, run the one that's stronger for their situation.

**Swing A — Testimonial (use when you have a real result from a similar business):**
> Here's why I bothered calling instead of just moving on. We fixed this exact thing for [similar local business / "a [industry] shop across town"], and they went from [X] bookings a month to [Y]. Same traffic, same town, one working site. The problem you've got right now is the one we just solved for them.

**Swing B — Demo line (use when you've already mocked up their site, or will for warm leads):**
> And honestly, I was so sure this was costing you customers that we already built a quick mockup of your site with it fixed. It's sitting ready to show you. Same brand, same look, except this version actually lets people book. I'd rather show you than talk at you about it.

**Combine when you have both (strongest):**
> We did this for [business] and they picked up [Y] bookings a month. I was confident enough it'd work for you that we already mocked up your version. Let me show you.

*(Note for ops: the demo line converts hardest but only if a demo can exist. You don't need one pre-built for every dial. Use the testimonial on the cold dial, and if they lean in, say "I'll have a mockup of your actual site ready on our call." Then build the demo only for booked, warm prospects. That keeps demo effort tied to real intent.)*

---

## Step 3 · Book the time (to show them live)

The demo/teardown is the reason to meet. Never send it cold, the whole value is walking them through it.

> Give me 15 minutes and I'll put your actual site up on screen, show you exactly where the leak is and what the fixed version looks like. You decide from there, no obligation. I've got [day] at [time] or [day] at [time], which is better?

**Lock it in:**
> Perfect. Best email for the calendar invite? ... Done. I'll have your site and the fixed version pulled up and ready. Talk [day].

---

## Objection handling

**"That's a weird way to call people / I don't appreciate the trick."**
> Totally fair, and I hear you. But I did it that way on purpose, because that flash of friction you just felt is exactly what a real customer feels right before they leave. If it bugged you for 10 seconds, imagine it bugging someone with their credit card out. That's the money I called about. Give me 15 minutes to show you the fix?

**"We already have a website."**
> You do, and it looks [genuine compliment]. But I just tried to [order / book] on it and couldn't, so the question isn't whether it exists, it's whether it's actually booking people. That's what I'll show you. 15 minutes.

**"How much is this?"**
> Rebuilds run $2k to $10k one time depending on scope, but that number only matters if the extra bookings cover it, which is the whole point of the call. Let me show you the leak and the fix first, then you'll know if the math works. [day] or [day]?

**"Just send me the demo / info."**
> I could, but it lands way better when I walk you through it live, and it's only 15 minutes. Half the value is me showing you where people drop off. [day] at [time] work?

**"I'm too busy."**
> That's why it's 15 minutes and I do the prep, not you. What's your quietest slot this week, [day] morning or [day] afternoon?

**"Not interested."**
> No problem, I'll leave you alone. Real quick though, so I learn, is it that the site's not a priority, or that you didn't love being called like that? [Listen, thank them, hang up warm.]

---

## After the call
- **Booked:** invite within 5 minutes. If you promised a demo, that prospect is now warm enough to justify building the mockup. Have it ready.
- **Callback:** call back exactly when you said.
- **Hard no:** log and move on.

## What to log for the A/B/C test
```
Dials:              ______
Conversations:      ______
Reveal reactions:   ____ positive  /  ____ neutral  /  ____ annoyed
Calls booked:       ______
```
Watch the **annoyed** count closely. B lives or dies on whether the hook reads as a favor or a stunt. If annoyed is high but booked is also high, keep it. If both annoyed and no-booked are high, the reveal is landing wrong, tighten it. Booked ÷ Conversations is the head-to-head number vs A and C.
