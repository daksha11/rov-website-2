# FLOW · THE SHELF — books that sharpen your taste (CTRL-A recurring column)

A simple, repeatable recommendations format for CTRL-A. Two editions share one template:
**THE SHELF** (books) and **THE KIT** (tools we've been loving). The "added value" is a fixed
two-part structure on every pick, so it's never just a list:
- THE TAKE — the one-line reason it matters (the hook)
- STEAL THIS — a concrete move the reader can use today (the value)

Topic: five books that sharpen taste. Thesis: the fastest way to close the taste gap is to borrow
taste from people who already have it. (Direct sequel to `ctrla-taste-gap-social.md`.)
Funnel job: Reach (highly saveable + comment bait: "what's on your shelf?"). CTA is soft: The Shelf
is a recurring CTRL-A column, more every volume, free.
ICP: intentionally broad (creatives everywhere), matching CTRL-A's brand.

CONFIRM BEFORE POSTING: these are a strong on-brand default shelf, not verified as the team's real
favorites. An "our favorites" post only lands if it's authentic. Swap any title for what the crew
actually loves. Placeholders to confirm are marked [confirm].

Deck: 1080x1350, 7 slides. Export as PDF for LinkedIn, individual images for IG.
