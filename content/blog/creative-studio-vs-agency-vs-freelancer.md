When you need creative work done, you have three options: hire a freelancer, hire an agency, or hire a creative studio. They are not the same, and picking the wrong one is where budgets and timelines go to die. This guide breaks down what each does best, when each is the right call, and how to decide for your Atlanta brand.

## The Short Answer

- **Freelancer** — one person, one craft. Best for a single, well-defined deliverable on a tight budget.
- **Agency** — coordinates specialists, often outsources production. Best for large, ongoing campaigns with a big budget.
- **Creative studio** — one team that designs and builds across disciplines in-house. Best when you need brand, web, and content to feel like one thing.

Range of View Studios is a full-service creative studio in Atlanta. This guide is written from that seat, but the breakdown is honest about when a freelancer or agency is the better call.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the difference between a creative studio and an agency?**

An agency usually coordinates a project and outsources much of the production to freelancers and contractors. A creative studio keeps design, development, and production in-house. With a studio you get fewer handoffs and one team that owns the whole outcome. With an agency you often get more scale but more layers between you and the people doing the work.

**Is a creative studio cheaper than an agency?**

Usually, yes. Studios carry less overhead than large agencies, so more of your budget goes into the actual work rather than account managers and office space. A studio is typically more expensive than a single freelancer, but you are paying for range and coordination that one freelancer cannot provide.

**When should I hire a freelancer instead of a studio?**

Hire a freelancer when you have one clear, contained deliverable, such as a single logo, one landing page, or one video, and a limited budget. When the project spans multiple disciplines or needs to stay consistent across brand, web, and content, a studio will save you the cost of managing several freelancers yourself.

**What is best for a small business in Atlanta?**

For most Atlanta small businesses that need a brand and a website that work together, a creative studio is the best fit. You get senior craft across disciplines without agency-level overhead, and one team accountable for the result. If you truly only need one small thing, a trusted freelancer can work.
