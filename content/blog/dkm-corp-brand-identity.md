## The Challenge: A Professional Services Website That Undermined Credibility

Dheeraj Kumar Miryala founded DKM Corp with a clear vision for his business, but the existing web presence did not reflect it. The site looked dated, lacked a cohesive **brand identity**, and did not communicate what DKM Corp actually did, or why someone should work with them.

This is a common problem for **professional services companies**. The founder is busy doing the actual work, and the website becomes an afterthought. It gets built once, maybe updated a few times, and then sits there slowly becoming less relevant. Meanwhile, potential clients are visiting the site, forming an impression in about 3 seconds, and bouncing if that impression does not hold up.

DKM Corp also had a logistical challenge: Dheeraj is based in India, and we operate out of Atlanta. The project needed a process that worked across a **10.5-hour time difference** without sacrificing the collaborative back-and-forth that good design requires.

## The Solution: Brand Identity Design and a Ground-Up Website Rebuild

We approached this as a ground-up rebuild, not a facelift. The old site was not worth iterating on. Starting fresh let us build a **brand identity** and website that worked together from the beginning.

**Discovery across time zones.** We started with deep discovery sessions to understand DKM Corp's business, audience, and competitive landscape. Working across Atlanta and India meant we had to be disciplined about communication. We used asynchronous design reviews. detailed annotated mockups, recorded walkthroughs, and structured feedback documents. so that progress never stalled waiting for a time zone to wake up. The 10.5-hour gap actually helped in some ways: we would send work at the end of our day, Dheeraj would review overnight our time, and we would have feedback waiting in the morning.

**Brand identity from first principles.** We did not just pick colors and fonts. We worked with Dheeraj to articulate what DKM Corp stands for, who their ideal client is, and what feeling the brand should evoke. From there, we built a visual system: logo refinement, color palette, typography, and graphic language that could extend beyond the website to presentations, documents, and social media. A brand is not a logo. It is a **brand identity system**, and we built the system.

**Modern responsive website design.** The rebuilt dkmcorp.in is fully responsive and designed for how people actually browse in 2026. Over 60% of global web traffic now comes from mobile devices. We designed **mobile-first**, then expanded for larger screens. Every page, every section, every call to action works on a phone screen without compromise.

**Clear messaging architecture.** Professional services sites often fall into the trap of saying everything and communicating nothing. We worked with Dheeraj to strip the messaging down to what matters: what DKM Corp does, who they do it for, and what makes working with them different. Each page has a purpose. Each section moves the visitor toward contact or inquiry.

**Professional credibility signals.** For a services company, the website is often the first real interaction a potential client has with the brand. We built in the trust signals that matter: clear service descriptions, professional photography direction, consistent visual quality, and a **web design** standard that communicates competence before anyone reads a word. First impressions on a website happen in under 50 milliseconds. The design needs to earn trust immediately.

## Key Takeaways for Professional Services Website Design

- **Your website is your first handshake.** For **professional services companies**, the website sets the tone for every client relationship that follows. If the site looks like it was built in 2018, potential clients will assume the work product is similarly outdated. DKM Corp's **website redesign** gave them a first impression that matches the quality of their actual services.
- **Time zones are a feature, not a bug.** The Atlanta-India collaboration worked because we designed a process around asynchronous communication. Structured feedback, annotated mockups, and recorded walkthroughs kept the project moving 24 hours a day instead of waiting for scheduled calls.
- **Brand identity systems outlast individual designs.** We did not just build a website. we built a **visual identity system** that DKM Corp can apply across every client touchpoint. Trends in web design shift every 2 to 3 years, but a strong brand system adapts without needing a complete overhaul.
