DKM Corp needed a website that matched the caliber of work they were doing. We rebuilt dkmcorp.in from scratch — a collaborative redesign between our Atlanta studio and founder Dheeraj Kumar Miryala in India.

## The Challenge

Dheeraj Kumar Miryala founded DKM Corp with a clear vision for his business, but the existing web presence did not reflect it. The site looked dated, lacked a cohesive brand identity, and did not communicate what DKM Corp actually did — or why someone should work with them.

This is a common problem for professional services companies. The founder is busy doing the actual work, and the website becomes an afterthought. It gets built once, maybe updated a few times, and then sits there slowly becoming less relevant. Meanwhile, potential clients are visiting the site, forming an impression in about 3 seconds, and bouncing if that impression does not hold up.

DKM Corp also had a logistical wrinkle: Dheeraj is based in India, and we operate out of Atlanta. The project needed a process that worked across a 10.5-hour time difference without sacrificing the collaborative back-and-forth that good design requires.

## The Solution

We approached this as a ground-up rebuild — not a facelift. The old site was not worth iterating on. Starting fresh let us build a brand identity and website that worked together from the beginning.

**Discovery across time zones.** We started with deep discovery sessions to understand DKM Corp's business, audience, and competitive landscape. Working across Atlanta and India meant we had to be disciplined about communication. We used asynchronous design reviews — detailed annotated mockups, recorded walkthroughs, and structured feedback documents — so that progress never stalled waiting for a time zone to wake up. The 10.5-hour gap actually helped in some ways: we would send work at the end of our day, Dheeraj would review overnight our time, and we would have feedback waiting in the morning.

**Brand identity from first principles.** We did not just pick colors and fonts. We worked with Dheeraj to articulate what DKM Corp stands for, who their ideal client is, and what feeling the brand should evoke. From there, we built a visual system — logo refinement, color palette, typography, and graphic language — that could extend beyond the website to presentations, documents, and social media. A brand is not a logo. It is a system, and we built the system.

**Modern responsive design.** The rebuilt dkmcorp.in is fully responsive and designed for how people actually browse in 2026. According to Statista, over 60% of global web traffic now comes from mobile devices. We designed mobile-first, then expanded for larger screens. Every page, every section, every call to action works on a phone screen without compromise.

**Clear messaging architecture.** Professional services sites often fall into the trap of saying everything and communicating nothing. We worked with Dheeraj to strip the messaging down to what matters: what DKM Corp does, who they do it for, and what makes working with them different. Each page has a purpose. Each section moves the visitor toward contact or inquiry.

**Professional credibility signals.** For a services company, the website is often the first real interaction a potential client has with the brand. We built in the trust signals that matter — clear service descriptions, professional photography direction, consistent visual quality, and a design standard that communicates competence before anyone reads a word. First impressions on a website happen in under 50 milliseconds, according to research from Carleton University. The design needs to earn trust immediately.

## The Results

This project was about building a professional foundation, not chasing a single metric. DKM Corp went from an outdated site that undermined credibility to a modern, cohesive web presence that accurately represents the quality of their work.

The redesigned dkmcorp.in gives Dheeraj a digital headquarters he can point clients to with confidence. The brand system we built extends to every touchpoint — when DKM Corp sends a proposal, shares a presentation, or posts on social media, the visual identity is consistent and polished.

The cross-continental collaboration also proved something we believe strongly at ROV Studios: great work does not require everyone to be in the same room. With the right process, a team in Atlanta and a founder in India can build something together that neither could have built alone.

## Key Takeaways

- **Your website is your first handshake.** For professional services companies, the website sets the tone for every client relationship that follows. If the site looks like it was built in 2018, potential clients will assume the work product is similarly outdated. DKM Corp's rebuild gave them a first impression that matches the quality of their actual services.
- **Time zones are a feature, not a bug.** The Atlanta-India collaboration worked because we designed a process around asynchronous communication. Structured feedback, annotated mockups, and recorded walkthroughs kept the project moving 24 hours a day instead of waiting for scheduled calls.
- **Brand systems outlast individual designs.** We did not just build a website — we built a visual identity system that DKM Corp can apply across every client touchpoint. Trends in web design shift every 2 to 3 years, but a strong brand system adapts without needing a complete overhaul.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does a website redesign process look like?

Our process starts with discovery — understanding your business, audience, and goals. From there, we move into brand identity work, wireframing, visual design, development, and launch. For DKM Corp, the entire process was collaborative, with Dheeraj involved at every decision point. We do not disappear for 6 weeks and come back with a finished product. You see the work as it develops and shape it with us.

### How long does a website redesign take?

Most of our redesign projects run 6 to 12 weeks from kickoff to launch. The DKM Corp project included brand identity work alongside the website build, which added scope. Timeline depends on how many pages you need, whether brand work is included, and how quickly feedback cycles move. Cross-time-zone projects like this one require a bit more buffer for asynchronous reviews.

### What should I look for in a web design agency?

Look at their actual work, not their sales pitch. Ask how they handle collaboration and feedback. Find out if they build sites you can manage yourself or if you will need them for every small update. At ROV Studios, we prioritize giving clients ownership of their site after launch. A good agency builds something that works for you long after the project ends.

### What is a collaborative design approach?

Collaborative design means the client is a partner in the process, not a spectator. For DKM Corp, that meant Dheeraj reviewed wireframes, gave feedback on visual directions, and shaped the messaging at every stage. We bring the design and development expertise. You bring the knowledge of your business and customers. The best results come from combining both, not from an agency working in isolation and presenting a finished product.
