## The Premise

Every business has a gap between the money it's making and the money it should be making. That gap is the revenue leak.

The leak shows up in different places:
- A restaurant where the ordering page had 132 views (should have had 91,000)
- A fashion brand with zero online presence (losing sales to people who couldn't find her)
- A professional services company whose website made them look worse than they are

ROV finds the leak. Then fixes it.

**That's the whole business.** Everything ROV does — websites, branding, AI automations, SEO, video, social — is a tool for fixing a specific kind of leak. We don't sell services. We fix holes.

---

## The Two Moves

Every leak falls into one of two categories:

### Move 1: Generate Leads (Get Eyes On You)
The business exists but people can't find it, or don't trust it when they do. Money is leaking at the top — before anyone ever calls.

ROV fixes this with:
- **Website** — built to convert, not just exist
- **SEO & GEO** — visible in Google search AND AI-powered search (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search)
- **Social Media** — Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, Meta
- **AI Automations** — outreach at scale, not one email at a time
- **Video & Media** — content that builds trust before the phone rings
- **Ads** — when the platform is ready to convert them

### Move 2: Secure Leads (Turn Eyes Into Money)
The business is getting attention but not closing it. People are interested and then gone. Money is leaking in the middle — after they find you, before they pay you.

ROV fixes this with:
- **Branding** — logo system, color palette, typography, design language. The 3-second judgment call goes your way.
- **Brand Strategy** — who you're talking to, what makes you different, why someone should pick you over the competitor down the street
- **Brand Guidelines** — a rulebook that makes everything repeatable and consistent
- **Website** — the platform that holds everything together
- **Email & SMS Marketing** — they convert, then come back. Build fans, not one-time customers.
- **AI Automations** — missed call text-back, post-job review requests, follow-up sequences that run without you

## Who We're Talking To

**Right now:** Atlanta small business owners and home services companies (HVAC, roofing, plumbing, landscaping) who are good at what they do but leaking money on the digital side.

**The person:** 35-55 years old. Built their business on word of mouth and referrals. Business is fine but not growing. Knows something is off but doesn't know what to fix. Has probably either (a) never had a website that actually worked, (b) wasted money on ads that didn't convert, or (c) looked at a competitor's site and felt embarrassed by theirs.

**What keeps them up at night:** Phone stops ringing. Losing jobs to a competitor who's worse at the actual work but better at looking legit online. Spending money on HomeAdvisor or Google Ads and not understanding why the leads don't close.

**What they want:** More calls. More jobs. More money. Not a "digital transformation." Not "brand elevation." More jobs.

**How to speak to them:** Plain language. Outcomes, not process. Show the result before you explain how. They don't care about AI or SEO or GEO — they care that their phone rings. Lead with that.

## The Voice

### Who's speaking
Suchet Konda. 21 years old, Atlanta. Thinks in money flows and systems. Builds things that work. Doesn't have patience for corporate fluff or vague promises. Speaks like a smart friend who happens to know where your money is going.

ROV's brand voice IS Suchet's voice — direct, specific, confident, Atlanta. Not polished-agency. Not textbook-marketing. Real.

### Tone
- **Direct.** Say the thing. Don't warm up to it.
- **Specific.** Numbers, timelines, client names, real results. No generalities.
- **Confident without arrogance.** We know what we're doing because we have proof. The proof does the bragging.
- **Outcome-first.** Lead with what changed for the client, then explain how.
- **Atlanta.** Rooted here. Know the market. Not generic "any city" content.

### Voice Examples

**DO:**
> "TheBando's ordering page had 132 views. After we redesigned their site, it had 91,000 in 139 days. No ads. Just fixing the path from 'hungry' to 'ordering.'"

> "If your website exists but isn't making you money, that's not a website. That's a revenue leak."

> "We don't pitch until we know where your gap is. If ROV is talking to you, it's because we found something."

> "You're a great plumber. But if someone searches 'HVAC repair Buckhead' at 9pm on a Saturday and you don't come up, you never got a shot."

**DON'T:**
> "In today's digital landscape, businesses need to leverage cutting-edge solutions to elevate their brand."

> "We provide holistic marketing services to help companies achieve their goals."

> "Our team is passionate about helping businesses grow through innovative digital strategies."

> "We'd love to partner with you on your digital transformation journey."

## Content Voice (for blog posts, social, emails)

Every piece of content should do at least one of these three things:
1. **Show proof** — a result, a before/after, a real client story
2. **Teach something specific** — how we found the leak, what we changed, why it worked
3. **Name the gap** — point at a thing businesses are missing that's costing them money

Open every post with either a result or a problem. Not context, not background, not "in today's world." The result or the problem, immediately.

**Required elements for every post:**
- At least one real number, date, or measurable result
- At least one Atlanta or Georgia geographic reference
- At least one real example (not hypothetical)
- Written like talking to a smart business owner, not a marketing audience
