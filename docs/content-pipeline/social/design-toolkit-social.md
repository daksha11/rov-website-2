## CAROUSEL SLIDES

**Slide 1 — Hook**
> Why does your brand
> look homemade?
>
> (small label: THE DESIGN TOOLKIT · ROV STUDIOS · swipe →)

**Slide 2 — The story**
> A landscaping owner in Decatur built her logo in Canva the week she registered her LLC.
>
> It looked fine on her phone.

**Slide 3 — The turn**
> Then a $40,000 client pulled her estimate up next to a competitor's and asked why her invoice looked like a birthday flyer.
>
> She didn't lose the job because her work was worse. She lost it because her brand planted doubt before she said a word.

**Slide 4 — The diagnosis**
> A brand looks homemade when it has a logo but no system.
>
> No defined colors.
> No consistent type.
> No rules for how the mark survives a truck door, a favicon, or a black-and-white invoice.

**Slide 5 — The kit, part 1**
> The 8 tools behind every brand we build:
>
> **Figma** · where the brand gets designed, together
> **Illustrator** · precision vectors for logos and icons
> **Photoshop** · retouching and compositing, the standard
> **Google Fonts** · 1,500+ free, web-ready typefaces

**Slide 6 — The kit, part 2**
> **Coolors** · palettes locked and exported in seconds
> **Mobbin** · real shipped apps as reference, not concept art
> **Blender** · a full 3D suite that happens to be free
> **Spline** · interactive 3D for the web, no code
>
> Half of these are free. The tools were never the expensive part.

**Slide 7 — Myth**
> MYTH: "A DIY logo maker gets you basically the same result as a designer."
>
> It gets you a logo. It doesn't get you a system.
>
> The system, the exact fonts, colors, and rules, is what makes everything you make next look like the same business made it.

**Slide 8 — The test**
> A real design process has 5 steps:
>
> 1. Research, before anything gets designed
> 2. Personality, defined in words first
> 3. Typography and color, in that order
> 4. The mark, stress-tested small, in b&w, next to competitors
> 5. A system delivered, not a single file
>
> A same-day logo drop skips four of them.

**Slide 9 — The takeaway**
> Customers read consistency as reliability.
>
> A homeowner comparing three quotes can't name a single design principle. But a logo that looks different on your truck than your website plants a doubt neither of you can explain.

**Slide 10 — CTA**
> The full Design Toolkit is live.
>
> Every tool. The myths that waste founders' money. The 5-step process behind every brand we ship.
>
> rovstudios.com/resources/toolkits/design
>
> Free brand audits for Atlanta businesses. Link in bio / comments.
